UFO Crash - Brookhaven National Lab.

This amazing case is featured on the May 20th,2009 episode of UFO Hunters.


South Haven County Park, November 24, 1992 UFO crash incident facts:

Video footage shown of a large fire of some unknown object. Mylar blankets seen being issued between personnel; used for dead bodies. Fire trucks on site. 2 day recovery of unknown object over a two day period; large parts reported by witnesses being driven away on military flatbed trucks. Road construction to widen sections of road out of this area.

Special access by Brook Haven Lab (cutting edge particle accelerator research/Relativistic Iron Collider/Nobel Prize level work) personnel to crash site reported by eyewitnesses...County police turned away at a later point by men in black jumpsuits...Large flash of light light sup the night sky for 10 seconds, reported by several residents...power blackout denied by local power company...Rhode Island witness showing video footage of several lights hovering in same spot (sometimes for hours) and seen flying in eratic patterns before plunging in sequence behind a treeline.

Pretty compelling stuff if you have watched the video footage of smoldering large crash site taken by county firemen and later left to MUFON investigators anonymously.
